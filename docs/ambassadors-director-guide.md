# Amana Ambassadors — Director's Guide

**Pilot window: 17 August – 11 September 2026** at MFIS Greenacre, MFIS
Beaumont Hills, Minaret Doveton and Minaret Springvale.

Amana Ambassadors rewards educators who bring new families into our
centres. Your job as Director is to make sure every record is accurate
before money moves. Everything happens on the **Ambassadors** page in the
dashboard (Growth section).

## How educators refer families

Every educator (and you) has a personal 6-character code and a QR poster —
both on the **Codes & QR** tab. When a family scans the QR or types the
link, they land on the parent sign-up page with the educator's code
attached. The enrolment form also asks every family "How did you hear
about us?" — choosing *One of our educators* asks them to name who.

When an enrolment lands at your centre during the pilot, a record appears
in your **Worklist** automatically. Children are shown by initials only.

## What counts as a qualified enrolment

1. **A new child** — no enrolment at ANY Amana centre in the prior 6
   months. The system checks this automatically; when it can't be sure it
   asks you to confirm (the "Confirm new child" flag). Two siblings are
   two records.
2. **At least 4 paid sessions within 28 days of first attendance.** The
   free first session counts as attendance (it starts the 28-day clock)
   but a $0 session **never** counts as one of the 4.

## Your weekly jobs

- **Enter sessions.** Attendance lives in OWNA, so either add paid
  sessions on each record (open it from the Worklist) or ask admin to
  upload the weekly OWNA attendance export on the Admin tab. Watch the
  **Approaching day 28** filter — those records run out of window soon.
- **Set sessions per week.** Each credited record needs the child's
  regular sessions per week before you can verify it: **3 or more = $50**,
  **1–2 = $30**. You can change this right up until you verify.
- **Resolve conflicts.** If the QR code and the named educator point to
  different people, the record is flagged — speak with both and credit the
  right person. The system never picks for you.
- **Verify.** Once the details are right, press **Verify**. Verification
  locks the tier and sends the record to your State Manager for approval.

## What happens after you verify

State Manager approval → payroll export (run by admin) → paid. The
incentive appears on the educator's pay as "Ambassador Incentive"
(super is paid on top).

Two things to know:

- **No incentive is payable until the educator has completed all 3 units
  of the Amana Ambassadors course** in My Training. The dashboard blocks
  payroll for non-completers — remind your team early.
- An enrolment nobody is credited for **still counts toward your centre's
  target**; it just pays no individual incentive.

## The team bonus

When your centre reaches its qualified-enrolments target, the overview
shows **"$200 team pool earned"**. State Managers arrange that payment
separately — nothing more for you to do.

## Extras

- **Milestone kicker:** an educator's 5th qualified enrolment in the pilot
  earns them a one-off $100.
- **Rejecting a record** always needs a written reason, and every action
  is kept in the record's history.
